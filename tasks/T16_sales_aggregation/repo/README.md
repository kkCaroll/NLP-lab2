# saleskit

Small utilities for normalizing sales records and building revenue reports.

## Modules

- `saleskit.money`: decimal amount conversion and formatting.
- `saleskit.records`: record validation and normalization.
- `saleskit.aggregate`: regional totals and revenue summaries.
- `saleskit.filters`: record filtering and ordering helpers.
- `saleskit.metrics`: refund rate, average sale and regional share.
- `saleskit.report`: text and dictionary reports.
